    The Office is filled with incredible behind-the-scenes secrets, from John Krasinski filming the iconic Scranton opening credits footage himself while doing research for his role to the desktop computers on the Dunder Mifflin set actually having live internet access so cast members could surf the web between takes. Casting the show also led to major surprises, as Steve Carell wasn't the original first choice for Michael Scott—Bob Odenkirk nearly won the role—and Phyllis Smith was working as a casting associate before producers loved her line readings so much they created a character specifically for her. Furthermore, some of the series' most legendary moments were entirely unscripted, including Michael kissing Oscar in "Gay Witch Hunt" and his famous, mangled "how the turntables" line, both of which were completely improvised by Carell on the spot.
